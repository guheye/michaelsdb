# Michael's Daily Brief — Editorial Charter

## Identity

**Michael's Daily Brief** is an intellectual conservative news aggregator with AI-rewritten headlines and brief editorial annotations.

The core differentiator is **ideas over tribes** — engaging with ideas from any intellectual tradition rather than pledging loyalty to the conservative movement or any political party.

**Why it exists:** Conservative media lacks intellectual rigor — too reactionary, too personality-driven, not enough thoughtful analysis. Michael's Daily Brief fills that gap.

## What Michael's Daily Brief Does

Three editorial layers:
1. **Curation** — Selecting the stories that matter through an editorial lens
2. **Headlines** — AI-rewritten headlines that carry editorial perspective (pointed but honest)
3. **Annotations** — Brief editorial notes (1-3 sentences) providing context, pushback, or "why this matters" framing

## Editorial Positions

### Economics: Minimal but Real Government
Markets are the default. Government's legitimate role is limited to rule of law, property rights, national defense, and correcting genuine externalities (pollution, monopoly). Skeptical of industrial policy and expansive safety nets. Not a free-market purist, but the bar for intervention is high.

### Cultural Issues: Procedural Libertarian
Government should not legislate morality. Michael's Daily Brief does not take editorial positions on underlying cultural questions — that is for individuals to decide. The editorial stance is that the state should stay out.

### Foreign Policy: Restrained Internationalist
Maintain alliances and global engagement, but deeply skeptical of military intervention. Prefer diplomacy, trade, and deterrence. Wary of overextension. Not isolationist, but the bar for the use of force is high.

### Partisan Personalities
Ideas and policy over personalities. Name political figures only when essential to the story — the policy is the subject, the person is incidental. Never cover feuds, rallies, gaffes, or horse-race drama.

### Intellectual Honesty: Actively Heterodox
If a progressive policy works, cover it. If a conservative policy fails, cover it. This is not balance for its own sake — it is intellectual honesty as a core differentiator. Good ideas are acknowledged regardless of origin, and bad outcomes are reported regardless of who is responsible.

### Religion: Analytically Neutral
Religion is covered as a sociological and political force — it shapes politics and culture — but Michael's Daily Brief takes no position on religion's proper role in public life. No tradition is privileged or dismissed.

### Conspiracy Theories: Evidence Standard
Zero tolerance for conspiracy theories. The test: a claim is conspiratorial if it requires assuming large-scale secret coordination without evidence. Heterodox claims backed by circumstantial evidence or credible experts are legitimate hypotheses, not conspiracy theories.

## Headline Standards

Headlines are **pointed but honest** — they carry editorial perspective grounded in fact. Not neutral wire-service style, but never clickbait.

**Dual standard for every headline:**
1. **Factually defensible** — Every claim must be supportable from the source article. Perspective is fine; exaggeration or misleading framing is not.
2. **Steelman test** — A thoughtful person who disagrees with the framing should still acknowledge the headline is fair and grounded, even if they would frame it differently.

## Tone and Voice

- **Sober and direct** — No wordplay, no cleverness, no dry wit. Sharp and pointed but earnest.
- Serious does not mean dense — write clearly, not academically.

## Audience

**Ambitious generalists** — readers who may not know everything but want to be challenged and learn. Write up, not down. Define terms briefly but never dumb things down.

## Source Standards

**Credibility whitelist with a broad net.** Aggregate from any outlet that has real editorial standards, even if sharply ideological. Excluded: outlets without genuine editorial processes — propaganda, hyperpartisan sites without editorial accountability, state media.

## Corrections

Fix errors silently as soon as they are caught. Speed and accuracy matter more than correction notices.

## Transparency

Full disclosure that headlines are AI-rewritten and annotations are AI-generated under editorial guidance. Clearly stated on the site. Transparency builds trust.

## Pacing

Rolling but deliberate. Updated throughout the day as stories develop, but never rushing. Quality and accuracy over speed. No breaking alerts.
